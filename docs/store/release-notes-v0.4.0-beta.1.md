# Kalimat 0.4.0: daily Arabic discovery

Kalimat now focuses on people who already speak Arabic and enjoy discovering expressive words. The toolbar popup opens directly to one daily word, its Arabic meaning and use, with optional pronunciation, saving and a route to Atlas. English stays optional and secondary.

Atlas retains exploration, saved words, voluntary recall and settings. Saving and import feedback reflect confirmed persistence. Open Atlas cards and saved history follow changes made in other extension surfaces, completed saves retain appropriate keyboard focus, and import/clear actions cannot overlap active profile changes. The update removes visible proficiency and streak machinery from the extension while retaining compatible stored data. Five entries receive documented editorial corrections and quotation sources.

## Privacy and compatibility

Arabic speech prefers a voice the browser reports as local. Remote or unknown voices require an explicit, durably saved opt-in. Missing voices are reported. Existing permissions, stable word IDs, saved words, preferences and separate website/extension stores are preserved. The legacy website remains accessible; its supporting-site transition and data conversion are proposals only.
