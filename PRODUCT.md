# Kalimat

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People who already speak Arabic and want interesting words or unfamiliar meanings worth knowing. The first evaluator is the maintainer, Assem.

## Product Purpose

Offer a satisfying Arabic encounter in under a minute during ordinary browser use. Success means enjoying the encounter, remembering some words, and recognizing or using them later. Opening a tab or saving a word does not prove learning.

## Operating Context

The approved first milestone is a 14-day pilot. Everyone receives the same entry for the day. The popup opens a reading page that shows the full encounter until dismissed, then a compact reminder for that day. The store package keeps existing new tabs unchanged. A separate local build can opt in to new-tab replacement. Users can reopen the encounter, save words, and take occasional skippable recall prompts without a backlog.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Use the existing vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript extension. Preserve existing saved words, history, and separate legacy website storage. Do not add accounts, sync, telemetry, streaks, or personalization. Delivery works from approved bundled entries without requiring network access. Occasional batch approval supplies future content. Revisit approved encounters when the reserve ends. Newly written examples also require review.

## Editorial Standard

Familiar words qualify through a genuinely new meaning, useful distinction, or concrete image. منوال and شجون are the agreed benchmarks. Contemporary Arabic leads, with occasional literary discoveries. Distinguish documented meanings from historical derivation. Do not invent word histories or quotations.

The user approved these fourteen entries: منوال، كظم، شجون، محك، أناة، فتيل، نقد، حذو، شغف، سجال، حرج، إبرام، استهلال، سبر. Their examples are original editorial writing. User approval does not constitute independent linguistic certification.

## Brand Commitments

Keep the Kalimat name and Arabic-first presentation. Use clear, concise copy and let layout and interaction explain the interface. Preserve useful accessibility and recovery text.

## Evidence on Hand

The planning conversation contains the approved explanations, examples, and dictionary references. The existing extension provides Atlas, saved history, review, backup, and data deletion. The legacy website is a separate surface. Current implementation and verification details belong in CONTEXT.md and the development and verification documentation.
